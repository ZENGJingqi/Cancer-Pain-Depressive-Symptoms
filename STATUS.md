# Scientific status as of 7 September 2026

Four HRS Final Core endpoint models have now completed in an independent human-operated run. Fifty iterations and 20 imputations per model were used. Fifty-six pooled RR/risk/RD rows passed independent aggregate arithmetic verification; the maximum checked classic split R-hat was 1.01488 and maximum MCSE/SE was 0.0796%. These are limited diagnostic findings, not blanket validation. Five distribution/model combinations remain suppressed, private prediction QA is unreviewed and 15 console warnings have not been individually classified.

The complete manuscript below is still v0.9, predating the Final Core update. New sample sizes differ; HRS extensions, multicohort synthesis and figures require reconciliation. New Poisson-standardized absolute risks are not replacements for the logistic-standardized manuscript table. Death status remains based on archived RAND tracking.

The manuscript includes the existing onset and persistence analyses, absolute risks, pain-burden and functional-adjustment analyses, and aggregate direct and composite comparisons. HRS direct and composite results were returned from the user's independent local run; aggregate arithmetic checks passed. That does not establish full model validity or imputation convergence.

## Outstanding before a submission-ready release

1. SHARE official PSU and stratum sensitivity: national design mapping, matched analytic sample and variance comparison remain unfinished. The returned design preflight is not this model.
2. Full multiple-imputation convergence and observed-versus-imputed distribution review. Short chain summaries and small Monte Carlo error alone are insufficient.
3. Independent freezing and verification of non-HRS composite pre-model datasets.
4. The HRS 2022 version decision and four core endpoint updates are complete. Synchronization of all dependent analyses, sample flow, acknowledgements and the manuscript remains incomplete.
5. Author list, affiliations, funding, conflicts, contributions, institutional ethics determination, applicable statistical review statement and final STROBE checklist.

Comparisons between symptom-only and death-or-symptom composite analyses change samples, weighting and missing-data handling. They cannot isolate a causal effect of survival selection. CHARLS cancer strata are small. HRS and SHARE do not validate cancer-site-specific claims. The current SHARE primary design is the documented approximation, not a model using the newly downloaded official PSU/stratum fields.

References are the 26 references retained from the preceding manuscript; this release is not a new systematic literature search. Uploading this review version is not journal submission or final scientific approval. No new participant-level models were run to assemble this repository.
