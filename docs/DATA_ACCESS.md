# Data products used in this study

Obtain the study data from their providers under the applicable registration
and use terms. The repository does not redistribute participant data.

## Health and Retirement Study HRS

RAND HRS Longitudinal File 2022 Version 1 and RAND Fat Files for 2012–2022 were
used for the longitudinal source framework. The eight depressive-symptom items
at the last follow-up were updated using HRS 2022 Final Core Version 2.0.
Earlier intervals and baseline variables remain RAND-based; mortality remains
from the archived RAND source, not a newly downloaded Final Tracker.

- [RAND products and documentation](https://hrsdata.isr.umich.edu/data-products/rand)
- [HRS 2022 Core release and access](https://hrsdata.isr.umich.edu/node/58262)
- [HRS data portal and registration](https://hrsdata.isr.umich.edu/)

## Survey of Health Ageing and Retirement in Europe SHARE

Gateway Harmonized SHARE Version G, based on SHARE Release 9.0.0, and the
corresponding official longitudinal weights were used. Source waves are 5, 6,
8 and 9. Main intervals are 5–6 and 8–9; 6–8 is a sensitivity interval.
Official PSU/stratum sensitivity was not conducted in this study.

- [Registered-user access](https://share-eric.eu/data/data-access)
- [Release documentation and identifiers](https://share-eric.eu/data/data-documentation)
- [Gateway harmonized products](https://g2aging.org/harmonized-data/overview)
- Wave DOIs: [5](https://doi.org/10.6103/SHARE.w5.900),
  [6](https://doi.org/10.6103/SHARE.w6.900),
  [8](https://doi.org/10.6103/SHARE.w8.900),
  [9](https://doi.org/10.6103/SHARE.w9.900),
  [longitudinal weights](https://doi.org/10.6103/SHARE.wXweights.900).

## China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study CHARLS

Original waves 2011, 2013, 2015 and 2018 were used. Harmonized CHARLS Version D
supported cross-wave variable mapping; core measures were checked against
original wave files. CHARLS 2020 was not included.

- [Official data portal](https://charls.charlsdata.com/)
- [Harmonized CHARLS and links to original waves](https://charls.charlsdata.com/pages/Data/harmonized_charls/en.html)
- [Gateway harmonized products](https://g2aging.org/harmonized-data/overview)

NHIS is not part of this longitudinal study. The products above identify the
versions actually used, not a recommendation to substitute the latest releases.
